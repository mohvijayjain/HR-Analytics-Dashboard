# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **employee attrition** using **HR data**.
The goal is to identify **why employees are leaving the organization** and understand the **key factors contributing to attrition**, using data cleaning, transformation, and interactive dashboards built in **Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition trends
* Identify major reasons for employee turnover
* Understand impact of factors like:

  * Job role
  * Salary
  * Job satisfaction
  * Work-life balance
  * Overtime
  * Department
* Help HR teams take **data-driven retention decisions**

---

## 🗂 Dataset

* **Source:** HR Analytics CSV dataset
* **Records:** Employee-level HR data
* **Key Columns:**

  * Attrition
  * Age
  * Department
  * Job Role
  * Monthly Income
  * Job Satisfaction
  * Work Life Balance
  * OverTime
  * Years at Company
  * Education
  * Gender

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation (Power BI)

Performed using **Power Query Editor**:

* Removed unnecessary columns
* Handled missing and inconsistent values
* Corrected **data types** (especially Attrition column)
* Converted categorical fields into usable formats
* Standardized column names for clarity
* Created calculated columns & measures

---

## 🧮 Measures & Calculations

Some important measures created:

* Total Employees
* Total Attrition Count
* Attrition Rate (%)
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Years at Company

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

* **Total Employees KPI**
* **Attrition Count & Attrition Rate**
* Attrition by:

  * Department
  * Job Role
  * Gender
  * Salary Slab
  * Years at Company
  * Job Satisfaction
* Interactive slicers for:

  * Department
  * Gender
  * Job Role

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Higher attrition observed in employees with:

  * Low job satisfaction
  * Overtime work
  * Lower salary range
  * Early years in the company
* Certain job roles and departments show higher turnover
* Work-life balance plays a major role in employee retention
